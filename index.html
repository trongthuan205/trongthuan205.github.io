Trong-Thuan Nguyen


AILab, University of Science, VNU-HCM

I am an instructor in the Faculty of Information Technology and a researcher at AILab, University of Science, VNU-HCM, where I am also a Ph.D. candidate in computer science.

My research focuses on video understanding, video reasoning, and multimodal AI, with broader interests in generative AI and applied computer vision.

I served as a team lead at Saigon AI Hub (SAIH), Google Labs’ first applied AI lab in Vietnam, where I worked on multimodal reasoning and soccer video question answering.

Research

My research focuses on structured representations for visual understanding, video reasoning, multimodal reasoning, and image and video generation.

Video understanding
Scene graphs and hypergraphs for modeling objects, relationships, and temporal dynamics in video.
Video reasoning
Reasoning about events, temporal relationships, and interactions in video, including video question answering.
Multimodal reasoning
Multimodal language models for reasoning about visual scenes and answering questions about video content.

Future interests

I am open to exploring Quantum AI, Physical AI, and cybersecurity for AI systems in future research.
